High 5 Rotate

High five. Rotate. Keep dancing.

An Android partner rotation timer for dance teachers. Play a song in your usual music app, hear cues to switch partners, and pause near the song's end.

Choose your timings, a built-in sound or your own audio file. No High 5 Rotate account, ads, or analytics.

The app is being prepared for testing and Google Play publication.
