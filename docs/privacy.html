High 5 Rotate privacy policy
Updated: 8 September 2026

High 5 Rotate helps dance teachers time partner rotations while music plays in another app. It has no account system, advertisements, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and does not request internet access.

Media and notification access
Android calls the permission used to discover music players “notification access.” This permission is broad, but High 5 Rotate uses it only to discover active media sessions, read the song title, artist, duration and playback position, and send playback controls such as pause. The app does not read notification message contents. Track information is processed on your device and displayed in the app and its ongoing notification. It is not saved as a listening history or transmitted by High 5 Rotate.

Your settings and audio files
Timing preferences, sound choices, and whether the timer is armed are stored locally. If you select an audio file, the app stores its display name and a reference with permission to read that file. The file is played locally and is not uploaded by High 5 Rotate. If you choose a cloud-backed document, your selected document provider may download it under that provider's own terms and privacy policy.

Background activity
While you arm the timer, a foreground service monitors playback. A CPU wake lock is held while timing music or playing a cue, so the screen can stay off. Disarming stops the service and releases audio and CPU resources. Battery settings remain under your control.

Data collection and sharing
High 5 Rotate does not collect or share personal data off your device. It does not sell data or send information to the developer. Other music apps, document providers, Android services, and Google Play have their own privacy practices. The app disables Android automatic backup; some device manufacturers may still transfer local settings when you migrate devices.

Your control
You can disarm at any time, revoke notification access in Android settings, select a different sound, or clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove its locally stored preferences. Android manages the permissions for selected files. No High 5 Rotate account exists to delete.

Contact and updates
The project is maintained at https://github.com/floitsch/high5rotate. For questions about privacy, open an issue at https://github.com/floitsch/high5rotate/issues. Issues are public, so do not include sensitive information. Changes to this policy will be published in the repository and included in future app updates.
