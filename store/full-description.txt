Teach the class. Let high5rotate keep track of partner changes.

high5rotate is a partner rotation timer for West Coast Swing and other partner dance classes. Start a song in your usual Android music app, and high5rotate plans dance blocks around the song's remaining duration.

HOW IT WORKS
• Arm the timer once for class.
• Play songs in a compatible music app, such as YouTube Music or Spotify.
• Hear a cue when it is time to change partners while the music plays quietly.
• Let high5rotate request a pause near the end of the song, then choose the next song yourself.
• Disarm when class is over.

MAKE IT FIT YOUR CLASS
Choose your target dance-block length, minimum and maximum block lengths, minimum final block, and switch time. Choose a built-in cue or your own audio file, or turn tones off while keeping the quiet switch interval. An optional final cue signals a partner change after the song.

The timer continues while you use another app or lock the screen. For reliable timing, allow high5rotate to run without battery optimization in your phone's settings.

MEDIA ACCESS AND PRIVACY
Android calls the required permission “notification access.” high5rotate uses it to discover music players, read track timing, and request playback controls. It does not read notification message contents, collect a listening history, or send your information to the developer. No high5rotate account, ads, or analytics are included.

COMPATIBILITY
Android 8.0 or newer is required. The music player must expose an Android media session; playback controls and audio ducking depend on the player and device. When track duration is unavailable, high5rotate provides fixed-interval cues but cannot reliably stop before the next song. Adjust the end guard on your actual phone and Bluetooth speaker.

high5rotate does not supply music and is not affiliated with the music services mentioned above. Any account or subscription required by your music player is separate.
